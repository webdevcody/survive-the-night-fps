# The weekly full run

Once a week, play the game the way a group of friends would, start to finish, and write down everything that got in
the way. Features ship fast; this is the regular check that each one still helps the night instead of diluting it
(issue #277). The base is only there to keep the team alive long enough to reach the objectives, so watch whether
the run keeps moving toward the escape.

## The rules

- [ ] A fresh game on the **default difficulty** (Nightfall), from the main menu, on the live build.
- [ ] **Real players**, two or more if you can get them, on their own machines. Watch them more than you talk.
- [ ] **No admin**: no `/give`, no `/day`, `/dusk` or `/dawn`, no god mode, no dev menu. If you need one to get
      unstuck, that is the first annoyance.
- [ ] Play to the end: the escape, or the wipe. Nobody leaves early to "check something".
- [ ] Everyone fills in the end screen: how hard it was, the best moment and the worst moment.

## While playing

Write down, with the minute it happened:

- every time someone asked "what do I do now?" or "where is…?"
- every time someone waited with nothing to do (a menu, a crafting queue, a long walk with nothing on it)
- every death that felt unfair, and what killed them
- every time the team split up for something that was not the objective
- lag, rubber-banding, sounds out of place, things clipping
- what made people laugh or shout, so it is kept

Afterwards, file **each annoyance as its own issue** (one problem per issue, with the minute and who hit it). Players
are usually right that something is a problem and often wrong about the fix: write the problem, not their fix.

Add a line per run, newest first: the date, the build, who played, how it ended, the issues filed.
